Meet Claudio

Not a superhero. Just human.

Claudio is curious, kind and optimistic — and gloriously imperfect. He doesn’t lecture anyone. He watches everyday life, makes mistakes, asks simple but meaningful questions, and discovers lessons through ordinary encounters.

You’ll know him anywhere: spiky black hair, an expressive face, a casual T-shirt, jeans, sneakers — and, more often than not, a backpack on his shoulders.

  • Curious
  • Kind
  • Optimistic
  • Imperfect
  • Open-minded
  • Naturally funny

Meet Max

Small dog. Big silences.

Max is Claudio’s small dog and constant travel companion. He never says a word — he doesn’t need to. Through a tilted head, a stubborn sit or a perfectly timed nudge, Max brings the humor, the emotion, and sometimes the wisdom Claudio missed.

Sometimes the quietest one in the room understands the most.

The journey

Different places. The same heart.

Claudio travels through different places and cultures, meeting people of every background, ethnicity, religion, belief and generation. Along the way, one thing keeps showing up: underneath our differences, we share the same…

  • hopes
  • fears
  • struggles
  • dreams
  • need for connection
Themes

What the stories are about

Emotional, dramatic or touching — always with intelligent, natural humor.

Love

The kind we give, lose, find again and learn from.

Friendship

Strangers who become something more in a single afternoon.

Family

What we inherit, what we pass on, and what we choose to change.

Kindness

Small gestures that quietly change someone’s day.

Peace

Between people, between neighbors, and within ourselves.

Happiness

Often found where nobody thought to look.

Tolerance

Seeing the person before the label.

Nature

Our responsibility to the planet we borrow from our children.

Human behavior

Why we do the strange, funny, beautiful things we do.

Visual style

Simple lines. Timeless feeling.

A simplified 2D hand-drawn world, built to be recognized at a glance and understood in any language, by any generation.

  • Clean, expressive black lines and minimal detail
  • Faces and body language you can read instantly
  • Cinematic composition — room for silence
  • Warm, natural colors when color is used
  • A timeless look that works across cultures
