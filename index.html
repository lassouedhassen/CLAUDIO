FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Claudio?

Claudio is a platform that helps you create, organize and share your work in one simple place.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan is free forever and includes all the core features.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. There are no long-term contracts — upgrade, downgrade or cancel whenever you like.

How is my data protected?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. You can export or delete your data at any time.
