Meet Claudio

A curious mind. A kind heart. A global citizen.

Claudio is not a superhero and he never lectures. He is human: curious, kind, open-minded, optimistic, funny and sometimes clumsy. He watches everyday life, makes mistakes, asks simple but meaningful questions, and learns his lessons from ordinary encounters.

Meet Max

Small dog. Big silences.

Max is Claudio’s dog and constant travel companion. He never says a word, and he doesn’t need to. A tilted head, a stubborn sit or a long, pointed stare brings the humor and the emotion, and sometimes the wisdom Claudio missed.

Max is the quiet mirror of the series. Claudio usually loses the argument.

The journey

Different places. Same people.

Claudio travels through different countries and cultures, meeting people of every background, ethnicity, religion, belief and generation. Underneath our differences, we share the same hopes, fears, struggles, dreams and need for connection.

Episode 1

A Small Act, A Big Impact

  • Earth · Nature · Personal responsibility
  • 4–5 minutes
  • Cinematic · Emotional · Humorous · Hopeful

A beautiful morning in a coastal park. A plastic bottle rolls across the path. Claudio sees it, and walks right past. Someone will pick it up. Max stops, sits down next to the bottle and stares at him.

Claudio: “Don’t look at me like that.”

Claudio: “Fine. You win.”

One bottle becomes two, then ten. A little girl is watching. Her father is watching her. A cyclist stops, a café owner joins in, and two kids turn it into a competition that Claudio is badly losing. Then, at the shoreline, the mood changes…

“The Earth doesn’t need one perfect person. It needs billions of people who care a little more.”

Visual style

Simple lines. Timeless feeling.

A simplified 2D hand-drawn world that can be recognized at a glance and understood in any language, by any generation.

  • Clean, expressive black ink lines and minimal detail
  • Faces and body language you can read instantly
  • Cinematic composition with room for silence
